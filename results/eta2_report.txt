THE POPULATION A PAPER ACTUALLY SAMPLES: one model per family
  Papers citing 'N models' usually take 1-3 per family. Drawing one member
  per lineage at random leaves, by construction, ZERO within-family variance
  to pool -- so the relevant question is how much variance a paper FORFEITS
  by treating its N as independent. Reported as the design effect.
