====================================================================================
design                                    v_ab clipped    v_ab RAW     MS_ab       MSE

per-arm, restricted to shared latents          0.04492     0.04492    0.5319    0.2624
SHARED positions                               0.00000    -0.00657    0.1806    0.2201

  CONFIRMED: the shared design's raw latent x arm component is NEGATIVE (-0.00657),
  i.e. MS_ab < MSE -- latents vary no more across arms than positions
  vary within a cell. Clipped to 0 for reporting.

  Direction of the resulting bias in E rho^2, which is easy to get
  backwards: v_ab is in the DENOMINATOR, so clipping a negative up to
  zero ENLARGES the denominator and SHRINKS E rho^2.
    E rho^2 clipped (reported) = 0.808
    E rho^2 unclipped          = 0.837
  So the clipped figure is the conservative one. The honest statement
  is still not a point estimate: a component pinned at a boundary is
  'not distinguishable from zero', and the interval below is what
  should be quoted.
