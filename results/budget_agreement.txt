latent sample independently, so the two arms overlap on 3 of 120
latents at 12M and agreement is not estimable from these files.

retrain with --ckpt_every (src/chain4.sh) and re-evaluate the
milestones on ONE shared latent list, as src/eval_arms.py does.

check is not in the paper.
