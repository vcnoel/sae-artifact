============================================================================================
  Prediction: per-arm and shared position sets overlap MORE for
  strong-rho latents, because where activation predicts effect the two
  selection rules nearly coincide.

gemma3-1b  WEAK rho               16                     0.383          
           -> Mann-Whitney                                         0.534

  If strong-rho overlap is higher, per-arm selection is near-random
  exactly where activation fails to predict effect, and that is why
  those latents gain the most from a fixed position.
