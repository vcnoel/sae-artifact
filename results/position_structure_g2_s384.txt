(b) IS THE RESIDUAL SYSTEMATIC IN POSITION, OR UNEXPLAINED?
====================================================================================
  R^2 of residual on rel_pos                          = 0.0010
  R^2 of residual on log_act                          = 0.0001
  R^2 of residual on act_rank                         = 0.0014
  R^2 of residual on rel_pos + log_act + act_rank     = 0.0025
  Spearman(rel_pos, residual)  = -0.030 (p=0.006)
  Spearman(act_rank, residual) = -0.044 (p=4.8e-05)

  INTERPRETATION: the residual is position-within-cell BY CONSTRUCTION
  (it is the deviation from the latent x arm cell mean, and position is
  the only thing varying inside a cell). What the R^2 above shows is how
  much of it is SYSTEMATIC in observable position covariates. A low R^2
  means position matters enormously but not monotonically in depth or
  activation -- which position, not how late or how strong.
